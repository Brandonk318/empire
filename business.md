# The AI Empire Project — Business File

## Company
Mind at Ease Solutions LLC (Texas LLC), Fort Worth, TX. Brand: The AI Empire Project.
Owner: Brandon Kerney. Domain: theaiempireproject.com
AI-first platform business for small contractors, tradespeople, and small service businesses.

## Launch sequence
1. AI Office: proves the tech
2. Mind At Ease Bookkeeping: proves charging (90-day pilot)
3. Community sites YEE Academy + Groundwork: proves scaling
Trade Circle is the community backend all of these feed into.

## Products
- AI Office: AI tools for small service businesses (calls, leads, office work). Not fully live yet. Built on the Momoempire CRM.
- Bookkeeping: AI does the books, a human bookkeeper reviews. Free limited tier (no card) -> $39.99 trial with 2 weeks free -> $99/mo "Momo" tier.
- Bookkeeping add-on with AI Office ("Add your books", shown on the AI Office pricing page): Books Light $10/mo, Books Medium $20/mo, Books Heavy priced by volume.
- Trade Circle: networking community and free trade education for contractors and artisans, internationally. The Academy is education, NOT certification. Extras: Cheers reward points (noncashable), merch, custom apparel, design-rights marketplace.
- Groundwork: reentry pathway community. YEE Academy: young-entrepreneur sibling brand.
- Shared Credit Tank: one prepaid balance across Empire sites; minimum top-up around $2.50 (proposed).
- Branded prepaid mobile (MVNO): exploring only; wholesale quotes requested.

## Fees and money rules
- Marketplace seller fee: 5% licensing, 10% outright sale. Default license cap $33.
- Referrals: qualify on 4th paying referral (per site); no back pay. Share of what each referral actually pays, net of refunds: 25% months 1-6, 10% months 7-12, 2% months 13-24, 1% months 25-36; bonus 1% months 37-48 for referrals locked in at 250 active paying referrals.
- Commissions are kept separate from credits and Cheers. Weekly payouts.
- Target free-to-paid conversion: about 10%.

## Who we sell to
Small contractors, tradespeople, artisans, and small service businesses.
Priority markets: US, India, Brazil, Indonesia, Nigeria, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Mexico, Vietnam, Philippines, plus Trinidad/Caribbean.
Partners: trade schools, polytechnics, and workforce agencies.
