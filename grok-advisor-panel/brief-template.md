# Brief template (what you give the panel)

The panel can only be as specific as the brief. Gaps you leave become `[GAP]` points,
which is fine, but filling them in first makes the packet far more useful.

```
# Brief: <decision in one line>

**Company / context:** <one line: what you sell, to whom, stage>
**Decision:** <a yes/no or A/B/C question, with a date>
**Options:** A) …  B) …  C) …   (include "do nothing / not now" if it's real)
**Mode:** quick | normal | strict

**Numbers we have** (with source and period)
- Revenue / MRR, growth:
- Burn, cash in bank, runway:
- Churn (logo and revenue), by segment if relevant:
- Unit economics relevant here (price, margin, CAC, payback):
- Capacity: who would do the work, and what they'd stop doing:

**Numbers we DON'T have:** <list them; the panel will treat them as GAPs, not guesses>

**Customer evidence** (see reference/evidence-guide.md)
- Source type and count (interviews / calls / tickets / win-loss), segment, dates:
- Verbatim quotes with an ID (#3, ticket 4411, …):

**Constraints:** <legal, timing, contracts, people>
**Reversibility, my first guess (1–5):**
**What I'm worried I'm missing:**
```
