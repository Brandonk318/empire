# Decision log (fill in AFTER you've decided)

One entry per decision. Copy the block, fill it in on the day you decide, and put the
30- and 90-day review dates in your calendar straight away. Keep the file
append-only: don't rewrite old entries. Add review notes under them.

Adapted from the two-layer decision memory in alirezarezvani/claude-skills
`decision-logger` and the journal in harshilmathur/autodecision (both MIT; see `vendor/`).
