# Brief 01: Pricing change (per-location tiers)

**Company:** Tallyhouse, bookkeeping and sales-tax SaaS for independent restaurants (US).
**Decision:** Do we replace the $49/month flat price with per-location tiers on March 1?

**Proposed tiers**
| Tier | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $39 / location / mo | Bookkeeping, 1 user |
| Pro | $79 / location / mo | + sales-tax filing, 5 users |
| Group | $149 / location / mo | + multi-entity consolidation, unlimited users |

**What we know (from our own systems, last 90 days)**
- 412 paying accounts, 571 locations total. ARPA $52 (some legacy add-ons).
- 78 accounts have 2+ locations; they hold 237 of the 571 locations.
- Logo churn 3.1% per month; churn among multi-location accounts 1.4% per month.
- Gross margin 81%.
- Support tickets tagged "sales tax" were 22% of volume last quarter.
- Sales-tax filing is currently a free beta used by 164 accounts.

**Customer evidence**
- 11 recorded interviews from September (transcripts in shared drive; 3 quotes pasted below).
  - "Honestly the tax filing is the only reason I haven't gone back to my accountant." (single location, Austin)
  - "Paying per location would hurt. We have four and two of them are tiny kiosks." (4 locations, Denver)
  - "I'd pay more if it actually handled multi-state. Right now I still do Colorado by hand." (2 locations)
- Win/loss notes on 19 lost deals: 7 cite price, 6 cite missing POS integration.

**Constraints:** Grandfathering for 6 months is on the table. Billing system supports per-seat and per-location.

**Options:** A) roll out tiers March 1; B) tiers for new customers only; C) keep flat price, charge $25 for sales-tax filing.
