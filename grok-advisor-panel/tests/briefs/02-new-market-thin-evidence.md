# Brief 02: New market, Canada (THIN CUSTOMER EVIDENCE)

**Company:** Tallyhouse, bookkeeping and sales-tax SaaS for independent restaurants (US).
**Decision:** Do we launch in Canada (Ontario + BC first) in Q2?

**What we know**
- 3 inbound demo requests from Canadian restaurants in the last 6 months (2 Toronto, 1 Vancouver). None converted; we couldn't serve them.
- Our founder had one dinner conversation with a Toronto restaurant group owner who "would definitely try it."
- Engineering estimate for GST/HST/PST support: 10-14 weeks for 2 engineers.
- We have no Canadian entity, no Canadian bank account, no French-language UI.
- Current US growth: 4.2% MoM net new MRR.

**Customer evidence:** Nothing beyond the above. No interviews, no surveys, no Canadian
pipeline. A competitor (named in Slack as "the big one") reportedly launched in Canada last year.

**Options:** A) build Canadian tax support and launch Q2; B) run a 60-day demand test
(landing page + 15 interviews) before committing; C) not this year.
