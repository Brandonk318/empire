# Brief 04: Product sunset, payroll add-on

**Company:** Tallyhouse, bookkeeping and sales-tax SaaS for independent restaurants (US).
**Decision:** Do we shut down the payroll add-on (launched 2024) by end of Q1?

**What we know**
- Payroll add-on: $15/location/month. 37 accounts (9% of 412) use it; $780 MRR.
- It's built on a third-party payroll API whose price goes up 40% in April.
- Payroll causes 31% of P1 support incidents (wrong tips allocation, late filings).
- 1.5 engineers spend roughly half their time maintaining it.
- Accounts with payroll churn at 1.9%/mo vs 3.1%/mo for the base.
- A partner (Gusto-style payroll provider) offered a referral deal: $40 per referred account plus 10% rev share for 12 months.

**Customer evidence**
- 6 of the 37 payroll accounts were called last week by the CS lead. Notes:
  - 4 said they'd move if given 90 days and help migrating.
  - 1 said "if payroll goes, we go" (3 locations, $192/mo total).
  - 1 didn't pick up.

**Options:** A) sunset end of Q1 with partner migration; B) sunset end of Q2 with longer runway; C) keep it and raise price to $29.
